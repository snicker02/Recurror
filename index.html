✺ Recurror
Eraser

Cuts lines where it touches them, through any symmetric copy; the pieces stay editable. Fills are left alone.

Shape

Drag to draw. Shift: circle, square or 15° steps · Alt: from the centre. Shapes use the current brush and symmetry and edit like any stroke.

Lasso

Selects every stroke with a copy inside the loop (Shift adds).

Fills the loop with the Bucket's fill and symmetry settings, under the layer's lines.

Bucket (fill tool)

Click a closed area to fill it, or click on a line to recolour the line itself (its shading stays). Click a fill again to restyle it; select it with Edit to delete it.
